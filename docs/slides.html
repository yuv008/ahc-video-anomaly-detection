Anomalies are contextual, so the pipeline is temporal A small VLM decides windows; a cheap aggregator decides events. Nothing large runs at inference.

STAGE 0 Window sampler 4 s windows, no overlap, 8 frames each. Second 32 s pass for classes defined by persistence. 865 windows / 56 min video
STAGE 1 Gate (optional) Frozen SigLIP + linear head on 1–2 frames. Drops background before the VLM sees it. throughput multiplier
STAGE 2 Qwen3-VL-8B · 4-bit LoRA LoRA, vision tower frozen. Emits minimal JSON — no prose, because the labels are the prose. 4.64 s/win @8f · 4f for real-time
STAGE 3 Temporal aggregator Hysteresis on P(anomaly) from token logprobs, then merge. The model never predicts timestamps. CPU, free to re-tune

Why the aggregator owns the timestamps

Training clips are pre-trimmed: the event fills 99.9% of the median clip. Real Level-2/3 footage is 7.7% anomaly. A model trained naively learns “a clip was shown to me ⇒ something is wrong” and fires continuously on ordinary traffic. So window labels come from temporal overlap, not the folder a clip sits in — which recovered 1,604 genuine background windows from inside anomaly clips.

Zero-shot catches what looks dramatic — fire, flood — and misses almost every contextual class: congestion, stalled vehicle, blocking, wrong-way, loitering, fighting. Exactly the distinction the brief calls the hard part, and exactly what the training windows teach. The gap between 0.329 and 0.964 is the classifier, not the pipeline: the plumbing already reaches the ceiling with a perfect model.

Optimise F1 at IoU 0.1 official gate is IoU 0.5, per-video wrong objective 1 false alarm on a normal L2/3 video scores it 0. Silence beats a guess, and we were tuning the wrong number.

What we would do next

Timing is the ceiling, not classification. Even a perfect window model scores 0.78 on L2 timing because 4 s boundaries cannot align to real events — boundary refinement is the remaining headroom. Level 3 stays out-of-distribution: its events reach 125 s while no training clip exceeds 30 s, and stitching cannot manufacture that.
